A relay is about twenty lines that forwards the request and adds the CORS headers the provider omits. Your key still travels from this page and is never stored by the relay. If you have not set one up, use Anthropic instead.
Whichever you pick, the key stays in this tab's memory only - never storage, never anywhere but that provider. Ticking this box is the only thing that sends your photos off the machine; untick it and they never leave.
    This report is not saved. It lives in this tab's memory only. Reloading or closing destroys it. Copy what you need or use Export Markdown first.

    What is measured and what is judged. Sharpness (variance of the Laplacian), exposure and highlight clipping, contrast, colourfulness, horizon tilt, background clutter, subject placement and near-duplicate distance are measured from your pixels - real numbers, shown next to every verdict so you can disagree with the weighting rather than a black box. The 1-10 scores and the winner are judged: they apply documented thresholds and per-platform weights to those numbers. That is a defensible heuristic, not ground truth, and it says nothing about what a photo is of. Only the optional caption writer understands subject matter.

    Data policy. With captions off, nothing leaves your browser - there is no server behind this page. With captions on, the chosen photo and its measurements are sent to whichever provider you picked (integrate.api.nvidia.com or api.anthropic.com); that is a real hop, and "we don't store it" is a claim about this page, not about every provider in the chain. Their retention and training terms are theirs, not mine to promise - read them if the photos are sensitive. Nothing here writes to localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or cookies, ever.
